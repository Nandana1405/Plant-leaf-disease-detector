DAY 9 - TRANSFER LEARNING CONCEPTS

1. Feature Extraction / Freezing

In feature extraction, the pretrained ResNet18 backbone is frozen.
The pretrained layers keep the features learned from ImageNet, such as
edges, shapes, textures, and patterns.

Only the new classification layer is trained for our four leaf disease
classes.

Freezing is useful when:
- The dataset is relatively small.
- We want faster training.
- We want to avoid overfitting.
- The pretrained features are useful for the new dataset.


2. Fine-Tuning

In fine-tuning, some of the pretrained layers are unfrozen and trained
on the leaf disease dataset.

Usually, the final ResNet blocks are unfrozen while the earlier layers
remain frozen.

Fine-tuning is useful when:
- We want the model to learn disease-specific features.
- The new dataset differs from the original ImageNet dataset.
- Feature extraction alone is not giving sufficient accuracy.

Training Plan:
Step 1: Load pretrained ResNet18.
Step 2: Freeze the backbone.
Step 3: Replace the final FC layer with a 4-class classifier.
Step 4: Train the new classification head.
Step 5: Unfreeze the final ResNet block (layer4).
Step 6: Fine-tune it using a smaller learning rate.
